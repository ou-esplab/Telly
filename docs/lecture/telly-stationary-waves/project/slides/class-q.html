What Is in Q?

Q = Qrad + Qlatent + Qsurface

ProcessWhat it isIn Telly
RadiationSunlight absorbed, infrared emitted: warms the tropics, cools the polesNot computed; the pull toward the background temperature stands in for it
Latent heatingHeat released when water vapor condenses into rainPrescribed from observed rainfall: the Q/cp term
Surface heatingWarm land or ocean heats the air above it by mixingNot computed; carried by the background temperature (the land–sea contrast)

So far we have thought of Q as radiative heating. In Telly, Q is the latent heating only.

Telly has no solar heating or outgoing longwave radiation, so no energy balance: the equator-to-pole temperature contrast is imposed by the background state, not produced.
