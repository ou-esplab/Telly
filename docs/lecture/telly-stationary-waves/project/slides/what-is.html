What Telly Is

PropertyDescription
The physicsThe class equations for motion, heat and mass, without the simplifications
The gridBoxes about 2° wide (about 200 km), in 26 layers up to about 2 hPa
What it tracksVorticity, divergence, temperature and surface pressure
Simple stand-insFriction, smoothing, a pull toward a background state, and heating from prescribed rainfall patterns
Left outDetailed treatment of radiation, clouds, the boundary layer and the land surface
