Is the Whole the Sum of the Parts?

What to look for

  • Where does the combined response differ from the sum of the three?
  • Is that difference small compared with each single response?

What we see so far

  • The combined response differs from the sum of the three
  • Large in northern winter over the North Atlantic, Arctic and North Pacific
  • Mountains plus heating and land–sea do not simply add

Preliminary: about 3.75 years after spin-up; winter and summer; 500 hPa height; runs still in progress
